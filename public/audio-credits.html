Radio: played from Egyptian Radio's official YouTube channel

These episodes are not copied into the game. They play inside YouTube's own embedded player (shown on screen), from the official channel of Egyptian Radio (الإذاعة المصرية, @EgyRadio), which enables embedding. They need an internet connection. The episodes are undated and are not presented as broadcasts from the game's dates.
